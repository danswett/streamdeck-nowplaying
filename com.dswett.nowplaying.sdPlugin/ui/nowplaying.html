Automatic follows whatever is actually playing, which is usually what you want. Pin a specific player only if you keep more than one open at once — a pinned player that is not running shows nothing rather than controlling something else.

Per-player volume uses the Windows app mixer. A player that is paused has no mixer entry, so the dial falls back to system volume until it resumes.

Which players work?

Any player that reports to Windows media controls — the panel that appears when you press a media key. That covers Spotify, TIDAL, Plexamp, Apple Music, Media Player and anything playing in Edge, Chrome or Firefox, including Plex and TIDAL on the web.

It only sees audio playing on this PC. Casting from Plex or Plexamp to a speaker, phone or another machine will not appear here, because nothing is playing locally to report it.
